**Elements Divided Update 45 – Campaign Update**
**Version 827**

*Changelog*
```
- Added Campaign Mode
	- Play through a series of story-driven Co-Op Missions
	- Found under Cooperative in the Play menu
	- Beat a Campaign to unlock its next Difficulty
- Added 4 Campaigns
	- Fire in the Machine
		- SufferCorp's machines are tearing through the city. Fight through their operation and shut the reactor down.
	- Undertow Conspiracy
		- A robbery at the beach leads into SufferCorp's hidden conspiracy. Follow the trail and bring it into the open.
	- Relics of the Living Desert
		- SufferCorp is hunting an ancient desert artifact. Protect the people in their path and keep the relic safe.
	- The Wind Against Steel
		- SufferCorp is raiding the temples. Defend the shrines and drive their champions out.
- Added 2 Unlockable Campaigns
	- Boss Rush
		- Fight every boss in one battle
		- Unlocked by beating all 4 Campaigns on Normal or higher
	- The Old Master
		- The Old Master wants to test your power himself
		- Unlocked by beating Boss Rush on Normal or higher
- Added 2 new Bosses
	- Bloodbloom
	- Subelement Specialist
- Added Campaign Music
- Added Boss spawn sound effect
- Adjusted Co-Op Difficulty
	- Higher Difficulties now add more enemies and faster attacks instead of mostly more Health and Damage
- Improved Co-Op Enemy AI
	- Enemies spread their attacks across players
	- Enemies telegraph their attacks
	- Enemies path around walls
	- Reworked attacks and movement for several enemies
- Optimized Co-Op enemy performance
- Reverted Halloween Sky
- Added a Forcefield effect to the Casual 1v1 board separators when you get close
- Fixed a bug when leaving a Co-Op Mission
- Fixed a bug with Death Zones in the Tutorial
- Fixed some errors with UI screens, Level Backups, and Quest Achievements
```
